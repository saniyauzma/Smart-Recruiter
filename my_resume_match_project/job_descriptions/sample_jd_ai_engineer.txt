Job Title: AI/ML Engineer
Company: TechNova Solutions
Location: Bangalore, India (Hybrid)
Experience: 2-4 Years

About the Role:
We are looking for a passionate AI/ML Engineer to join our growing team. You will be responsible for designing, building, and deploying machine learning models and AI-powered applications that solve real-world business problems.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, develop, and deploy machine learning models for NLP, computer vision, and recommendation systems
- Build and maintain scalable data pipelines for model training and inference
- Develop RESTful APIs using FastAPI or Flask to serve ML models in production
- Implement Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines using LangChain and vector databases
- Fine-tune large language models (LLMs) for domain-specific applications
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams including product managers, data analysts, and backend engineers
- Write clean, well-documented, and testable code following software engineering best practices
- Monitor model performance in production and retrain models as needed

Required Skills:
- Strong proficiency in Python (3+ years)
- Hands-on experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow
- Experience with LLM frameworks: LangChain, LlamaIndex, or Haystack
- Working knowledge of vector databases (ChromaDB, Pinecone, Weaviate, or FAISS)
- Experience with cloud platforms: AWS (SageMaker, Lambda, S3) or GCP (Vertex AI, BigQuery)
- Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, and CI/CD pipelines
- Strong understanding of NLP concepts: embeddings, transformers, attention mechanisms
- Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases (PostgreSQL, MongoDB)
- Excellent problem-solving and communication skills

Nice to Have:
- Experience with Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, or image generation models
- Knowledge of MLOps tools (MLflow, Weights & Biases, DVC)
- Published research or open-source contributions in ML/AI
- Experience with real-time streaming data processing (Kafka, Spark Streaming)

Qualifications:
- B.Tech/M.Tech in Computer Science, AI/ML, or related field
- 2-4 years of relevant industry experience
- Strong portfolio or GitHub profile demonstrating ML projects
